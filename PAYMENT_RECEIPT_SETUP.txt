Nitesh Academy - Payment Receipt Update

After extracting the project, run:

1. py manage.py migrate
2. py manage.py runserver

What was added:
- Payment and PaymentItem database tables
- Date of Birth field for new student registrations
- Detailed payment receipt after successful Razorpay payment
- Payment method, bank/wallet/UPI/card last 4 digits when Razorpay provides them
- Purchased course details and amount
- Print / Save PDF button
- Admin Dashboard > Payments history
- Secure receipt page available to the student who paid or a staff admin

Existing users may show "Not provided" for Date of Birth because older accounts did not store DOB.
